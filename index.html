Important definitions

Revenue is money from sales. Cost of goods sold uses the cost recorded for each item at the time of sale. Gross profit is sales revenue minus item cost. Estimated net profit subtracts recorded operating expenses from gross profit.

Accuracy depends on entering correct costs, sales, purchases, returns and expenses. This app does not automatically account for taxes, unpaid invoices or unrecorded costs.
